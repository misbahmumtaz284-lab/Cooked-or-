🔪 Catch the Killer

🎮 About the Game

Catch the Killer is a fast-paced arcade game where a masked killer is constantly on the run, and your mission is to catch him as many times as possible before the timer runs out.

Every successful catch earns you one point. The challenge is to react quickly, stay focused, and achieve the highest score possible before time runs out!

Rating: 4/5 ⭐

Catch the Killer offers an exciting and engaging experience with simple mechanics and a competitive twist. The countdown timer adds urgency, while the chase keeps players focused and entertained. Its straightforward gameplay makes it easy to understand but challenging to master.

🕹️ How to Play

1. Start the game and get ready to chase the masked killer.
2. Follow the killer’s movements and try to catch him.
3. Earn one point for every successful catch.
4. Keep catching the killer while the countdown continues.
5. When the timer reaches zero, the game ends and your final score is displayed.

🛠️ How the Game Was Made

The game is built around three core mechanics: character movement, collision detection, and a countdown timer.

* Character Movement: Allows the killer to move around while the player attempts to catch him.
* Collision Detection: Determines when the player successfully catches the killer.
* Scoring System: Increases the player’s score by one point for every successful catch.
* Countdown Timer: Limits the duration of each round and creates a sense of urgency.
* Game Over System: Ends the game when the timer reaches zero and displays the final score.

These mechanics work together to create a simple, interactive, and competitive gaming experience.

💡 What Makes the Game Unique?

Catch the Killer combines a suspenseful masked-killer theme with fast-paced arcade gameplay. Rather than requiring complicated instructions or advanced strategies, the game challenges players to rely on their reflexes, concentration, and movement.

The time limit adds another layer of difficulty, encouraging players to make quick decisions and maximise their score within a limited period.

🧠 Strategy and Planning

Success depends on how efficiently you chase the killer. Here are a few strategies to improve your performance:

* Stay focused: Pay attention to the killer’s movements and anticipate where he might go.
* Move efficiently: Avoid unnecessary movement and take the shortest available route.
* React quickly: Take advantage of opportunities to catch the killer without hesitation.
* Manage your time: Remember that every second counts, so avoid wasting time.
* Stay consistent: Focus on making repeated successful catches rather than taking unnecessary risks.

🏆 How to Win

The objective is to achieve the highest possible score before time runs out.

* Each successful catch earns 1 point.
* You can catch the killer multiple times during a round.
* The countdown timer determines the length of the game.
* When the timer reaches zero, the game ends and your final score is calculated.

The ultimate goal is to catch the killer as many times as possible, beat your previous record, and become the ultimate killer catcher!
